How pricing works

◇

Delivery price

The final delivery price is based on whichever is higher: the collateral-based rate or 1,000,000 ISK per jump.

◎

Route requirements

The specific origin and destination systems do not affect the price. The pickup location must be a public station, while the destination may be non-public.
