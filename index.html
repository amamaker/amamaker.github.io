How pricing works

◇

Delivery price

The calculator compares route-based and collateral-based pricing and uses the applicable amount for the selected service. Cargo size, delivery priority and delivery zone can change the final price. For eligible multi-contract Standard cargo, the calculator may offer a bundled price when it is cheaper than Heavy Cargo.

◎

Route requirements

The specific origin and destination systems do not affect the price. The pickup location must be a public station, while the destination may be non-public.

✎

Contract description

When creating the courier contract, specify Standard or Rush in the contract description so the delivery priority is unambiguous.

✦

Custom deliveries

Need to move cargo outside the standard limits or have a special request? Contact Amamaker via EVE Mail to discuss a custom delivery.
